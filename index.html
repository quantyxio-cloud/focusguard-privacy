Privacy Policy for FocusGuard

Package Name: com.focusguard.studyshield  |  Developer: Quantyxio  |  Effective Date: 26 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FocusGuard ("the App", package com.focusguard.studyshield) handles user and device information. FocusGuard is designed as a privacy-first, completely offline focus and digital wellbeing utility.

1. Summary: Zero Data Collection & Zero Sharing

  • No personal data collected: FocusGuard does not collect, store on external servers, sell, or transmit your name, email, contacts, messages, browsing history, or app usage logs.
  • No third-party sharing: FocusGuard contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics trackers, and no cloud servers. We do not share any data with third parties.
  • All processing stays on your device: All focus rules, timers, and app blocking happen locally on your Android device.

2. Permissions We Request & Why

To pause distracting apps and hide their notifications during an active Focus session, FocusGuard requests the following Android permissions strictly for core app functionality:

  • Installed Applications (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES): Used only to display your installed apps inside FocusGuard's app-selection list so you can choose which apps to pause during Focus Mode. This list is never transmitted off your device.
  • Usage Access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS): Used locally in real time during an active Focus session to detect whether the foreground app is one you chose to pause. Usage history is never recorded, profiled, or uploaded.
  • Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Used to show the full-screen Focus Shield overlay when you open a paused app during an active session.
  • Accessibility Service (BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE — Optional): Used solely to detect window-state changes (TYPE_WINDOW_STATE_CHANGED) for user-selected paused apps and return to the Home screen. FocusGuard sets canRetrieveWindowContent="false" and never reads screen text, keystrokes, passwords, or personal content.
  • Notification Access (BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE — Optional): Used solely to temporarily snooze/hide notifications from user-selected paused apps while Focus Mode is active, and restore them when Focus Mode ends. FocusGuard never reads, logs, or transmits notification message contents.
  • Notifications & Foreground Service (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, FOREGROUND_SERVICE): Used to display the persistent FocusGuard control bar in your notification shade so you can view remaining time, take a break, or turn off Focus Mode.

3. Local Storage

FocusGuard stores only your app preferences locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences (selected package names to pause, session duration, activity label, strict mode toggle, and local blocked-attempt counter). Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored preferences.

4. Children's Privacy

FocusGuard does not knowingly collect any personal information from children or any other users, as no personal data leaves the device.
